# Cosmological Strömgren sphere

This example follows a homogeneous, constant-temperature, matter-dominated
flat universe with a 400 ckpc spherical domain. The source turns on at
`z_i=9`, emits `5e48 photons/s` at `13.6 eV`, and the hydrogen mass density is
10 per cent of the total matter density. The front is defined by
`x_HI=0.5`.

The main figure plots `r_I/r_S` versus redshift. The black curve is the
recombination-only analytic solution. Magenta squares are the simulation
measurements; error bars are half the local radial cell width, divided by the
instantaneous proper Strömgren radius.
